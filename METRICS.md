# Metrics

## North Star Metric

Completed audits per week.

This metric best reflects whether users find enough value in the tool to finish the audit process.

---

# Input Metrics

## 1. Audit Completion Rate

Measures:
- how many visitors complete the audit form
- onboarding clarity
- UX quality

---

## 2. Share Rate

Measures:
- how often users share audit result URLs
- viral growth potential
- social distribution effectiveness

---

## 3. Email Capture Conversion Rate

Measures:
- how valuable users perceive the audit results
- lead generation effectiveness

---

# First Instrumentation

The first analytics events I would track:

- Landing page visits
- Audit form starts
- Audit completions
- Share button clicks
- PDF exports
- Consultation CTA clicks

---

# Pivot Metric

If audit completion rate falls below 15% consistently, it would indicate:
- poor onboarding
- weak value proposition
- confusing UX
- low perceived usefulness

At that point I would revisit:
- landing page messaging
- onboarding simplicity
- recommendation clarity
- visual trust signals

---

# Why These Metrics Matter

This is a lead-generation product rather than a daily-use SaaS platform.

Metrics should therefore focus on:
- completed audits
- conversion quality
- lead intent
- viral sharing

rather than traditional DAU/MAU metrics.
