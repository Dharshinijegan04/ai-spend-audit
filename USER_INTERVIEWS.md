# User Interviews

## Interview 1

### Person
A.K — Indie Hacker — Early-stage SaaS founder

### Quotes

- "We started paying for multiple AI tools without really tracking the overlap."
- "Most of our AI costs slowly accumulated over time."
- "I would definitely use a free audit tool before renewing subscriptions."

### Surprising Insight

The founder mentioned that AI spending decisions were usually made individually by developers rather than centrally by management.

### Design Changes

I improved the audit summary section to focus more clearly on team-wide optimization rather than individual plans.

## Interview 2

### Person
R.S — Freelance Developer

### Quotes

- "I switch between Cursor and Copilot regularly."
- "I don't actually know which subscription gives me the best value."
- "I mainly care about whether the tool saves time."

### Surprising Insight

The user cared more about productivity-per-dollar rather than raw subscription cost.

### Design Changes

I updated recommendation messaging to emphasize efficiency and workflow improvements instead of only savings.

## Interview 3

### Person
M.J — Student Startup Team Lead

### Quotes

- "We use free tiers until we absolutely need paid plans."
- "Most startup teams don't review AI costs monthly."
- "A benchmarking feature would be really useful."

### Surprising Insight

The team leader wanted industry comparison benchmarks more than just savings calculations.

### Design Changes

I added future benchmarking mode ideas into the roadmap and architecture planning.
