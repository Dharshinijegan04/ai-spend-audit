# Economics

## Lead Value Estimation

Credex earns revenue by helping companies purchase discounted AI infrastructure credits.

For this model, assume:

- Average customer spends: $5,000/year on AI tools
- Credex margin on credits: 15%
- Revenue per converted customer:
  - $5,000 × 15%
  - ≈ $750/year

This means each converted lead could realistically be worth between $500–$1000 annually depending on company size.

# Profitability Model

Assume:
- 5 customers/month
- $750/customer annual value

Monthly value generated:

5 × $750
= $3,750 annualized revenue added monthly

If acquisition costs remain below $500/month through organic growth, the tool becomes highly profitable.
