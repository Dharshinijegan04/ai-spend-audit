# Go-To-Market Strategy

## Target User

The primary target users for AI Spend Audit are:

- startup founders
- engineering managers
- technical leads
- small AI-first startup teams

The ideal customer profile is:
- Seed to Series A startup
- 5–50 employees
- spending heavily on AI tools
- using multiple subscriptions like ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor, and Copilot

These teams often move quickly and accumulate AI subscriptions without actively optimizing costs.

---

# User Intent

Users are likely to search for this tool after:
- noticing rising AI subscription costs
- budgeting for engineering expenses
- comparing AI coding tools
- reviewing SaaS expenses
- scaling engineering teams

Example searches:
- "reduce ChatGPT team costs"
- "best AI coding tools for startups"
- "AI SaaS cost calculator"
- "Cursor vs Copilot pricing"
- "Claude team pricing"

Most tools only analyze SaaS costs, but Credex can also provide discounted AI infrastructure credits as a direct solution.

This creates:
- a clear monetization path
